Quick Signing Tips

  • Ensure good lighting with your hand clearly separated from the background.
  • Face your palm steadily toward the webcam for ~0.5s so MediaPipe locks onto all 21 landmarks.
  • Watch the live feedback box in the Studio tab to monitor your accuracy.
  • In Indian Sign Language, some letters use two hands while others use one. This model classifies single-hand keypoint vectors.

MediaPipe 21 Hand Landmarks

The model extracts 21 anatomical keypoints per hand, computes relative displacements from the wrist (Point 0), and normalizes the vector to 42 float features.

Deep Neural Network Specs

Feedforward classification network with Dropout regularization trained on normalized coordinate vectors.

About Indian Sign Language (ISL)

Indian Sign Language (ISL) is the primary sign language used by millions in the Deaf and Hard of Hearing community across India. Unlike spoken languages, ISL communicates ideas, grammar, and words through spatial hand arrangements, finger configurations, and physical body orientations.

ISL vs Other Sign Languages

While American Sign Language (ASL) primarily relies on single-hand alphabet finger-spelling, traditional Indian Sign Language incorporates distinctive 2-hand structures for multiple characters (such as P, Q, X, Z) alongside single-hand signs. This system translates single-hand finger configurations and digit gestures seamlessly in real time.

Assistive Communication & Impact

By pairing computer vision with fast neural inference, this project bridges communication barriers in educational institutions, customer service counters, and healthcare facilities—enabling seamless two-way dialogue between signers and non-signers.
